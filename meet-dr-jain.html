A commitment to patient care

Helping patients live healthier lives.

Dr. Mamta Jain is a board-certified physician with more than 20 years of experience in internal medicine. Her mission is to provide leading-edge medical care to help her patients live healthier, more enjoyable lives.

Education & Experience

A broad background in internal medicine.

01

Medical Training

Dr. Jain graduated from medical school in India, completed her residency in internal medicine in New York, and completed her fellowship in critical care medicine at Albert Einstein College of Medicine in 2008.

02

Clinical Experience

Dr. Jain has over 20 years of extensive experience in inpatient and outpatient diagnostic and therapeutic medical management. This includes acute and chronic conditions for adult patients as well as preventive care.

03

Hospital Affiliation

Dr. Jain is affiliated with Memorial Hermann Hospital of the Woodlands and has been an active member in the Memorial Hermann Physician Network.

Beyond the office

Healthy living is personal, too.

Outside of patient care, Dr. Jain enjoys many principles of healthy living. Some of her favorite activities are yoga, meditation, sports, and cooking nutritious cuisine.

Married to an interventional cardiologist and a devoted mother of four children, Dr. Jain enjoys the rigors of both private practice and homemaking and deeply values the families of her patients.
