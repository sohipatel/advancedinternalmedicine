Before Your Visit

Important Telemedicine Information

Please review the following information before requesting a telemedicine appointment.

!

Medical Emergencies

If this is a medical emergency, call 911 or seek emergency medical care immediately.

$

Insurance & Payment

Your insurance will be billed for the telemedicine visit. You are responsible for any copay, coinsurance, and/or deductible required by your insurance plan.

+

When an In-Person Visit Is Needed

Your telemedicine provider may request an in-person follow-up visit before a diagnosis can be made. If an in-person visit is necessary, care will be provided as appropriate. Telemedicine charges still apply.

✓

Privacy

Your telemedicine session is private. Please choose a quiet and private location for your appointment whenever possible.

Why Telemedicine?

Care that fits your schedule.

Telemedicine can make accessing medical care easier when traveling to the office is difficult or unnecessary.

01

No Travel Required

Avoid transportation time and costs associated with traveling to the office.

02

Less Time Away

Telemedicine may reduce the amount of time you need to take away from work or other responsibilities.

03

Convenience at Home

Attend your appointment from a convenient, private location without the need for an office visit.

04

Family & Caregiving

Remote visits may make it easier to manage child-care, elder-care, and other responsibilities.

05

Less Waiting

A remote appointment can reduce the amount of time spent traveling to and waiting in an office.

06

Convenient Follow-Up

Telemedicine can provide a convenient option for certain follow-up visits and ongoing care.
