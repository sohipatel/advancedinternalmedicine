Welcome

Comprehensive care with a personal approach.

Dr. Mamta Jain, a board-certified physician in internal medicine, delivers expert medical care focused on improving her patients' quality of life.

Dr. Jain established Advanced Internal Medicine of the Woodlands in 2010 with a commitment to compassionately serving the residents of Montgomery County and their loved ones.

Her practice emphasizes preventative medicine, early detection, comprehensive evaluation, and collaborative treatment. Her multidisciplinary approach allows each patient's care to be considered as a whole.
